Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In brief: FREEDOM offers optional accounts. In guest mode, tracker entries stay in your browser on that device. When you are signed in, new check-ins are sent to FREEDOM and stored in its Cloudflare D1 database so they can be accessed across devices. Guest entries are not automatically uploaded when you sign in.

1. Information you enter

In guest mode, the recovery tracker stores the category, type of check-in, optional urge-intensity selection, optional trigger context, optional coping action and date in your browser local storage; these entries are not sent to FREEDOM. When signed in, check-ins and those optional structured reflections are sent to the FREEDOM API and stored in a Cloudflare D1 database, associated with your account. Account check-ins are returned only to the signed-in account. Avoid entering intimate descriptions, names or details that identify you or another person.

2. Device and browser access

The Beat the Urge five-minute exercise runs in your browser and does not send the selected trigger or exercise activity to FREEDOM. Guest tracker entries in local browser storage are not a secure medical record. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to view them. Clearing browser data may erase them. The clear-this-device control affects local browser data only; it does not delete account entries. Account entries are stored remotely and remain associated with your account when you change devices.

3. Technical information and hosting

The website and its server-side functions are hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the service. Account registration stores your email address, a password hash and salt (not your plain-text password), session records, and any optional name or mobile number you choose to add. Account check-ins are stored in the D1 database. FREEDOM does not intentionally collect payment details or use advertising trackers.

4. Community chat and reports

Community chat posts are public to visitors of the community page and display the nickname you choose, together with the message and posting time. Posts are stored in FREEDOM's database and associated with your account internally. Do not include your real name, contact details, address, explicit personal history or medical records. Automated checks screen some content but are limited and can miss harmful posts or incorrectly block acceptable messages. If you report a post, the message ID, your account association, the reason you provide and the report time are stored so an administrator can review it. Reports are not monitored continuously and this is not an emergency service. Administrators may hide or remove content that breaches the community guidelines.

5. Professional Network applications

The professional application form collects your name, email, optional phone number, applicant category, qualifications and training, optional professional registration body and number, experience, support areas, languages, location, service mode, availability, contribution type and short introduction. It also records your consent to the privacy notice and verification checks, optional session fee and (separately) your choice whether to be listed publicly and which contact method to publish. Applications are stored in FREEDOM's Cloudflare D1 database with a pending status for manual review. Submission does not mean approval or verification. Public profiles are shown only after manual credential review and explicit public-listing consent. If listed, the chosen email address or phone number is visible to website visitors; users contact professionals directly and arrange payment with them. FREEDOM does not collect session payments or commissions. Do not submit certificates, identity documents, patient records or sensitive personal information. Review evidence is recorded internally by the administrator.

6. External links

Some pages link to external organisations, including government and health-information websites. Those services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

7. Children and sensitive information

This website is general educational content and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit sensitive or identifying information through any external service linked from this site. Accounts are optional. Email verification and self-service password reset are not currently available. Do not reuse a password from another service. Account data export and account deletion controls are available in the account interface. Professional applicants can submit a withdrawal or profile-removal request at the withdrawal request page. Requests are reviewed manually. The administrator will verify the request through the original application email before deleting the applicant's application records and any associated public profile. The request record is minimised after completion.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when website features or data practices change. The date at the top will be revised when that happens. New collection or processing of personal information should be explained before the feature is launched.

AI assistant Ruby

Ruby is an AI-powered assistant that processes the message and a short recent chat history you submit to generate a response using Cloudflare Workers AI. The chat history is held in the page while the chat is open and is not saved to your FREEDOM account or written to the FREEDOM database by the Ruby endpoint. Cloudflare processes prompts to provide the AI service under its own service terms and privacy practices. Do not share names, addresses, contact details, passwords, explicit personal history or sensitive medical information. Ruby may make mistakes and is not a clinician, therapist or emergency service. If AI is unavailable, the chat may not respond.

Website feedback

When you submit the feedback form, FREEDOM stores your rating, feedback category, message, page path and submission time. If you are signed in, the submission is associated with your account internally. If you optionally provide an email address, it is stored with the feedback so the site team may reply. Feedback is visible to authorised administrators for service improvement. Public forms use hashed network identifiers for basic rate limiting; the raw IP address is not written into the feedback record.

Personal recovery companion

The companion uses the same tracker storage as the homepage. Signed-in check-ins are stored in your private account. Guest check-ins are stored in your browser. The goal you set on the companion page is stored locally in that browser on that device and is not synced to your account. The goal text is not sent to Ruby unless you choose to type it into the chat.

9. Contact

For professional application withdrawal or profile removal, use the private withdrawal request form. FREEDOM does not currently offer a dedicated privacy inbox. Do not send certificates, identity documents, patient records or other sensitive information through public comments or social media.

Important: FREEDOM is not a healthcare provider and the tracker is not a clinical record. For medical or mental health concerns, speak with a qualified professional.
