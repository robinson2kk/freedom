Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In brief: This version of FREEDOM is a static website. It does not ask you to create an account. Check-ins you save in the tracker are stored in your browser on your device and are not sent to a FREEDOM account or server.

1. Information you enter

The recovery tracker stores the category, type of check-in, optional urge-intensity selection and date in your browser's local storage. The tracker does not currently transmit those entries to FREEDOM. Do not enter names, contact details, explicit personal descriptions or other information that could identify you.

2. Device and browser access

Local browser storage is not a secure medical record. Anyone who can access your device or browser profile may be able to view the saved entries. Clearing browser data or site storage may erase them. Use the export feature if you want a copy, and the clear-data feature if you want to remove entries from that browser.

3. Technical information and hosting

The website is hosted using a third-party hosting provider. Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the service. FREEDOM does not currently operate its own analytics, advertising trackers, account system or server-side tracker database.

4. External links

Some pages link to external organisations, including government and health-information websites. Those services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

5. Children and sensitive information

This website is general educational content and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit sensitive or identifying information through any external service linked from this site. A future account, community or messaging feature would require additional safeguards and a revised policy before launch.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when website features or data practices change. The date at the top will be revised when that happens. If a feature begins collecting or transmitting personal information, the policy should be updated before that feature is introduced.

7. Contact

There is currently no dedicated privacy-request or support inbox for this project. Do not send sensitive personal information through public comments or social media. A contact channel and formal request process should be established before the website expands to accounts or community features.

Important: FREEDOM is not a healthcare provider and the tracker is not a clinical record. For medical or mental health concerns, speak with a qualified professional.
