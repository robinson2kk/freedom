Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In brief: FREEDOM offers optional accounts. In guest mode, tracker entries stay in your browser on that device. When you are signed in, new check-ins are sent to FREEDOM and stored in its Cloudflare D1 database so they can be accessed across devices. Guest entries are not automatically uploaded when you sign in.

1. Information you enter

In guest mode, the recovery tracker stores the category, type of check-in, optional urge-intensity selection and date in your browser local storage; these entries are not sent to FREEDOM. When signed in, new check-ins are sent to the FREEDOM API and stored in a Cloudflare D1 database, associated with your account. Account check-ins are returned only to the signed-in account. Avoid entering intimate descriptions, names or details that identify you or another person.

2. Device and browser access

Guest entries in local browser storage are not a secure medical record. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to view them. Clearing browser data may erase them. The clear-this-device control affects local browser data only; it does not delete account entries. Account entries are stored remotely and remain associated with your account when you change devices.

3. Technical information and hosting

The website and its server-side functions are hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the service. Account registration stores your email address, a password hash and salt (not your plain-text password), session records, and any optional name or mobile number you choose to add. Account check-ins are stored in the D1 database. FREEDOM does not intentionally collect payment details or use advertising trackers.

4. Professional Network applications

The professional application form collects your name, email, optional phone number, applicant category, qualifications and training, optional professional registration body and number, experience, support areas, languages, location, service mode, availability, contribution type and short introduction. It also records your consent to the privacy notice and verification checks. Applications are stored in FREEDOM's Cloudflare D1 database with a pending status for manual review. Submission does not mean approval or verification, and no public profile is created automatically. Do not submit certificates, identity documents, patient records or sensitive personal information. Where necessary, evidence should be requested separately through an appropriate secure process.

5. External links

Some pages link to external organisations, including government and health-information websites. Those services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

6. Children and sensitive information

This website is general educational content and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit sensitive or identifying information through any external service linked from this site. Accounts are optional. Email verification and self-service password reset are not currently available. Do not reuse a password from another service. Account data export and account deletion controls are available in the account interface. Professional applicants do not yet have a self-service application withdrawal or deletion control. Until a dedicated privacy contact and request process is published, applicants should avoid submitting information they may later need removed.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when website features or data practices change. The date at the top will be revised when that happens. New collection or processing of personal information should be explained before the feature is launched.

8. Contact

There is currently no dedicated privacy-request or support inbox or automated account-deletion workflow. Do not send sensitive personal information through public comments or social media. Until a formal request channel is published, do not use the account tracker for highly sensitive details or information whose deletion you may need to request.

Important: FREEDOM is not a healthcare provider and the tracker is not a clinical record. For medical or mental health concerns, speak with a qualified professional.
