When an urge shows up

Pause. Name what you're feeling. Change your surroundings, take a short walk, breathe slowly or contact someone you trust. Try delaying the decision for ten minutes.

Professional care matters

Support is part of strength.

For substance use, withdrawal concerns or behaviour that is disrupting daily life, contact a registered healthcare professional or addiction service. If alcohol dependence is possible, seek medical advice before stopping suddenly.

Explore WHO mental health resources ↗
Important health and safety note: FREEDOM provides general educational and self-reflection tools, not medical diagnosis or treatment. If you may be physically dependent on alcohol, do not stop suddenly without medical advice — withdrawal can be dangerous and sometimes life-threatening. Seek urgent medical care for severe shaking, confusion, hallucinations, seizures or other serious symptoms. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
