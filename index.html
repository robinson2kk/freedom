Build your recovery toolkit.

When an urge shows up

Pause. Name what you're feeling. Change your surroundings, take a short walk, breathe slowly or contact someone you trust. Try delaying the decision for ten minutes.

✧

Understand your triggers

Notice patterns such as stress, loneliness, boredom, social situations or certain routines. Curiosity helps you plan; blame rarely does.
