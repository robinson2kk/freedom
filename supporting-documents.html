FREEDOM

FOR EXISTING APPLICANTS

Submit supporting documents

If you have already submitted your FREEDOM application, use this page to share your supporting documents link. You do not need to fill in the application form again.

Documents to include

  • Education and qualification certificates
  • Professional registration or licence, where applicable
  • Relevant training certificates
  • Experience letters or relevant work records

Upload these files into one Google Drive folder, then copy its sharing link. Do not include identity documents or patient/client records. Set access to Viewer for anyone with the link only if you are comfortable sharing it with the review team.
