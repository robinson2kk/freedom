Call India's nationwide emergency response number. It connects people to police, fire and health-related emergency services.

Tele-MANAS — mental health support

Free, confidential, 24-hour mental health support from the Government of India. It can help with emotional distress, anxiety, stress, sleep concerns and guidance to further care. Multilingual support is available.

India's National Tobacco QuitLine provides counselling for people who want to stop using tobacco. The service also offers regional-language counselling through its network.

Important: alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous

If you drink heavily or may be physically dependent on alcohol, do not suddenly stop or attempt to detox alone. Contact a doctor or hospital for a safe plan. Severe shaking, confusion, hallucinations, seizures, collapse or other serious symptoms need urgent medical attention — call 112 or go to an emergency department.

If you are worried about harming yourself

If you may act on thoughts of harming yourself or cannot stay safe, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department now. If you can, stay with someone you trust and move away from anything you could use to hurt yourself. Tele-MANAS at 14416 can also provide mental health support, but it is not a replacement for emergency response.
