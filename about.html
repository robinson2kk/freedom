ABOUT FREEDOM

A fresh start, on your terms.

FREEDOM is a free, judgment-free educational space for people who want to understand their habits, make healthier choices and find support as they work towards change. We believe progress is personal, small steps matter, and asking for help is a strength.

Explore recovery pathsBrowse the video library
OUR PURPOSE

Support without shame.

Changing a habit can feel difficult, especially when stress, cravings, loneliness or setbacks are involved. FREEDOM aims to make reliable learning and practical self-reflection easier to access, without judgement or unrealistic promises.

WHO IT'S FOR

Every person taking a next step.

Whether you're thinking about changing a habit, already working on it, supporting someone you care about, or exploring ways to help others, you can use the educational resources at your own pace.

WHAT YOU CAN FIND

Tools and learning in one place.

  • Recovery paths and self-reflection guides
  • Optional progress check-ins and tracking
  • A free, English-first recovery video library
  • Community encouragement and shared learning
  • Information about finding qualified professional support
OUR APPROACH

Progress, not perfection.

We encourage realistic goals, curiosity about triggers, supportive routines and learning from setbacks. We respect that recovery looks different for different people, and that a lapse does not erase the effort someone has made.

OUR PRINCIPLES

What we want FREEDOM to stand for.

RespectNo shame, labels or judgement.
PrivacyBe thoughtful about the information you choose to share.
Responsible supportClear boundaries and encouragement to seek qualified care when needed.
Important: FREEDOM is an educational and self-reflection platform. It is not a medical service, emergency service, diagnostic tool or substitute for qualified care. If you may be physically dependent on alcohol, speak with a healthcare professional before reducing or stopping, as withdrawal can be dangerous. Seek urgent medical care for severe withdrawal symptoms.
