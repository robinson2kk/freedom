This library focuses on compulsive patterns that feel out of control or cause significant distress or disruption. Important safety note about alcohol: If you may be physically dependent on alcohol, do not stop suddenly or attempt a home detox without medical advice. Withdrawal can be dangerous and sometimes life-threatening. Seek urgent medical care for seizures, confusion, hallucinations or severe shaking.
