FREEDOM professional network

Apply to support someone's next step.

We want to connect people seeking support with responsible, qualified professionals and trained peer supporters. The network is intended to be respectful, transparent and safety-focused.

Who can apply?

Qualified professionalsPsychologists, counsellors, psychiatrists, doctors, addiction specialists and other appropriately qualified practitioners.
Trained peer supportersPeople with relevant training or experience who can offer non-clinical encouragement within clear boundaries.
Volunteer contributorsPeople who want to contribute time to educational content or community-support initiatives.
Paid service providersProfessionals who may offer paid consultations. They can optionally offer one or two free introductory sessions; this is not compulsory.

What the application will ask

  • Basic contact details and professional category
  • Qualifications, relevant experience and applicable registration details
  • Areas of support, languages and service location
  • Availability and whether you wish to volunteer, offer paid consultations, contribute educational content, or combine these
  • Your agreement to professional conduct, privacy and safeguarding expectations

Applications will be reviewed manually. Submitting an application will not guarantee approval. Where relevant, qualifications and registration details will be checked against the appropriate issuing body or official register. Only approved profile information will be considered for the public directory.

Applications are open for initial manual review. Submitting this form does not mean you are approved or verified. Do not upload or send certificates, identity documents, patient records or sensitive information. We may request appropriate evidence through a separate secure process before any professional profile is published.

Already applied? Request application withdrawal or profile removal.
